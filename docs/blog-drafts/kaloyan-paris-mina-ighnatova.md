# Kaloyan Mitov in Paris — shot by Mina Ighnatova

Paris still tests a face in a way few cities do.

Kaloyan Mitov’s Paris editorial for Velishe Model Management — photographed by **Mina Ighnatova** — is a study in quiet authority: open blazer, white tee, Eiffel Tower soft in the distance, and a gaze that doesn’t ask for permission. Black and white keeps the frame honest. No colour to hide behind. Just bone structure, posture, and city air.

Fashion week cities reward models who can hold stillness and motion at once. This set does both. It’s the kind of image that casting directors bookmark for catalogue, campaign, and international board conversations — not because it’s loud, but because it’s clear.
